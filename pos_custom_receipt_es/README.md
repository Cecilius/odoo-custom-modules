# POS Custom Receipt ES

Adapts the Point of Sale receipt to a Spanish simplified invoice
(*factura simplificada*).

## Features

- Adds a "Simplified invoice" title and removes the "VAT Ticket" prefix.
- Shows the company name and NIF (company registry) in the header.
- Adds a legal note that the simplified invoice does not replace a full
  invoice, and a closing "Thank you for your visit!" line.
- Renames "VAT" to "IVA" in the tax summary and removes the "Powered by Odoo"
  line.
- Places the invoice-request link below the QR code.

All texts are translatable; a Spanish translation is included.
