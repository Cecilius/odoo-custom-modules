# Short process description

## Webshop
- Customer places the order.
- If the order requires tax identification, checkout asks for NIF/NIE/VAT.
- The order is confirmed but remains pending invoice review.

## Back office review
- Open the sale order.
- Check country, amount, customer type, and tax identification.
- Decide whether the invoice remains simplified or must be full.
- Correct customer data if needed.
- Click **Approve Invoice Review**.

## Invoicing and shipping
- Create the invoice only after review.
- The module routes simplified invoices to the simplified journal automatically.
- Full invoices cannot be posted if required tax identification is missing.
- Shipping/invoicing should be done only after the review has been approved.
